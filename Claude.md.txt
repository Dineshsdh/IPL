📄 Product Requirements Document (PRD)
IPL Auction Platform (Realistic Simulation)
1. 🧭 Overview

The goal is to build a real-time, highly interactive auction system inspired by the Indian Premier League player auction.

This is not just a basic bidding app. It should feel like a live IPL auction room, including pressure, strategy, competition, and time constraints.

The platform enables users (teams) to bid on players, manage budgets, and build squads dynamically.

📌 Based on your initial draft:
This version expands it into a production-level PRD.
